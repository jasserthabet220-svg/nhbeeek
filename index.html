My Dearest Chahoudy

My love, every time I think of you, my heart feels warmer, softer, and more alive. You are not just a beautiful presence in my life; you are the calm in my storms, the smile in my silence, and the light I want to keep close forever.

I want to be the man who makes you happy in every season, who listens to your heart, who protects your peace, and who loves you not only in the good days but in every moment of life. You are my favorite dream, my sweetest thought, and my greatest blessing.

If I could choose forever, I would choose you. If I could hold on to one beautiful thing in this world, it would be your love. And if I could promise one thing, it would be this: I will love you with all my heart, with all my soul, and with all the time I have left.

You are my rose, my lily, my home, and my forever. I love you, Chahoudy, more than words can say, more than time can measure, and more than my heart can hide.

Forever yours, with all my love.
